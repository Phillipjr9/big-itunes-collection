Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your purchase. If you're not satisfied, follow the steps below to return or exchange within 7 days.

  1. Contact us via your Account or email hello@bigitunescollection.com.
  2. Include your order number and reason for return.
  3. Pack items in original condition and ship to our returns address.
  4. Once received and inspected, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with original tags attached. Final-sale items are not eligible for return.
