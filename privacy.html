Privacy Policy

This is a demo privacy policy for Big ITunes Collection. In this demo, no data leaves your browser — everything below describes how a production version of this policy would typically work.

Information we collect

Account details (name, email, delivery address), order history, wishlist items, and basic browsing preferences such as recently viewed products.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To personalize product recommendations and your wishlist
  • To send order updates and, where opted in, promotional emails
  • To improve site performance and prevent fraud

Cookies & local storage

This demo stores your cart, wishlist, and account session in your browser's local storage — no server or third party receives this data.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting hello@bigitunescollection.com.

Third parties

We do not sell personal data. Payment and delivery partners only receive the information required to fulfill your order.
